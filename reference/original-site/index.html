06 / TRUST IS EVERYTHING

Share your compute.
Not your computer.

UCompute is designed to put consent and boundaries first. Local workloads stay local by default. Trusted-device connectivity requires explicit authorization, with additional safeguards required before any public network launch.

See the project ↗
01

Local by default.

Choose to keep inference entirely on your own device.

↗
02

Permission, not assumption.

Trusted peers must be authenticated and explicitly authorized.

↗
03

Honest about the risks.

Remote operators may see processed data; network encryption alone isn't a privacy guarantee.

↗
04

Public compute needs stronger safeguards.

Untrusted peer execution stays gated pending isolation, abuse, and verification work.
